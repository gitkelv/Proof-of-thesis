      When does NEAR stop inflating?

      NEAR issues new tokens to validators every year. Since February 2026, the share of Intents fees NEAR captures buys NEAR back on the open market. The thesis pays off when buybacks outrun issuance.

      How it works

      1. Fetch

        Every day at 06:17 UTC a GitHub Action pulls Intents volume, fees, revenue and TVL from DefiLlama, stablecoin supply on NEAR, the NEAR price, and total supply and inflation straight from a NEAR RPC node.

      2. Measure

        One pure JavaScript file, model/thesis.mjs, turns the raw series into the metrics. The same file runs in CI, in the tests and in your browser.

      3. Grade

        Each claim is graded holding, weakening or broken against fixed thresholds. Two broken claims and the page says the thesis is broken.

      4. Publish

        The result is committed to the repo and deployed to this page. Today's reading is added to an append-only ledger.

      Questions

      Is this investment advice?

      No. It's an open model with its assumptions in plain sight. It tells you which parts of a common NEAR bull case the data supports today and which it doesn't. Do your own research.

      Why would a thesis page admit it's weak?

      A thesis that can't fail tells you nothing. Writing down the kill line in advance is what separates analysis from marketing. If the buyback claim is weak today, the page says so and shows how much growth would fix it.

      I disagree with a threshold. What do I do?

      Edit the CLAIMS list in model/thesis.mjs and run npm run live. Every grade on this page updates. Or move the sliders under Your numbers to test your own growth and capture assumptions.

      What does "Rerun it in your browser" do?

      It downloads fresh data from DefiLlama and a NEAR RPC node directly into your browser and runs the same model file locally. No server of ours sits in between, so you can check that the published grades aren't edited by hand.

      What are the known gaps?

      The buyback figure uses DefiLlama's holders-revenue series. Issuance uses the onchain maximum inflation rate, which slightly overstates real issuance when validators miss blocks. Past supply is estimated by rolling today's onchain supply back at the inflation rate. The projections hold the NEAR price flat.
